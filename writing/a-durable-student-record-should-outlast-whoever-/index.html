a durable student record should outlast whoever delivers the

Why durable student records improve handoff continuity and make personalization measurable in education.

TL;DR

A durable student record should preserve goals and what happened next, so continuity survives a handoff and personalization becomes measurable.

what should stay attached to a student record?

Keep the student’s goals attached to the record, along with what happened next.

That gives future lesson delivery the context needed to continue the student’s journey.

how does continuity survive a handoff?

Continuity survives when the record outlasts whoever delivers the lesson.

The next person can see the student’s goals and the events that followed, so the handoff carries useful context forward.

how can personalization become measurable?

Personalization becomes measurable when the student’s goals and subsequent outcomes remain connected in one durable record.

The same approach can be copied by any education operator.

FAQ
What should an education record include?

It should include the student’s goals and what happened next. Keeping both attached preserves context for future lesson delivery.

How can education teams preserve continuity during handoffs?

Use a durable student record that outlasts the person delivering the lesson. The next person can then continue from the student’s existing goals and history.

How does a durable record make personalization measurable?

It connects the student’s goals with what happened afterward. That connection makes the results of personalization easier to observe.
