a growing market gives you permission to test

A growing market permits testing, while changed learner behaviour in one narrow workflow provides the real demand signal.

TL;DR

A growing market gives you permission to test. Demand becomes clearer when one learner makes a better decision this week and behaviour changes.

what does a growing market actually tell you?

It tells you there is enough movement to justify a test. Market excitement creates permission to investigate an opportunity.

That signal still leaves the demand question open. Interest around a market does not show whether a learner will make a better decision in practice.

where should you look for the demand signal?

Pick one narrow workflow. Keep the question close to a real decision a learner needs to make.

The useful test is whether that learner makes a better decision this week. A focused workflow gives the test a clear place to look.

what receipt matters after the test?

The receipt is changed behaviour. A learner making a better decision provides evidence that the workflow affected what they do.

Market excitement is cheap because it can exist without action. Changed behaviour carries the demand signal forward.

how should builders interpret early excitement?

Treat excitement as permission to test, then move quickly toward observable behaviour. The question is whether the learner's decision improves within the chosen workflow.

That keeps the finding tied to use rather than sentiment. The market opens the door, and changed behaviour tells you whether the workflow matters.

FAQ
does a growing market prove demand?

A growing market gives you permission to test. It does not answer whether a learner will make a better decision in a real workflow.

what is a useful early demand signal?

Choose one narrow workflow and observe whether a learner makes a better decision this week. Changed behaviour is the useful receipt.

why focus on one narrow workflow?

A narrow workflow keeps the test tied to a specific learner decision. That makes changed behaviour easier to observe.
