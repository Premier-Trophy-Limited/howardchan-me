i’d put the AI tutor inside the teacher’s workflow

Why AI tutors earn trust when goals, notes, and next actions stay inside the teacher’s workflow.

TL;DR

AI tutors become easier to trust when they live inside the teacher’s workflow. Set the goal, save tutor notes in the same record, and keep the next action visible there.

where should an AI tutor live during a teaching session?

i’d put it inside the teacher’s workflow, where the session already has its context and follow-up.

That keeps the tutor connected to the work the teacher is already doing.

what should the teacher define before the session starts?

set the goal before the session. A clear goal gives the tutor and teacher a shared point of reference.

The goal also makes the session easier to understand after it ends.

what makes an AI tutor feel accountable after the session?

save tutor notes in the same record and make the next action visible there too.

That puts context and accountability in one place. That’s where trust starts.

FAQ
how do you build trust in an AI tutor?

Put the AI tutor inside the teacher’s workflow. Keep the goal, tutor notes, and next action connected in one place.

what should an AI tutor record after a session?

The tutor should save its notes in the same record as the session. The next action should be visible there too.

why should the session goal be set in advance?

A goal gives the session a clear direction. It also keeps the tutor’s work connected to what the teacher intended to accomplish.
